A little more about us

Make the moment feel personal.

Wish Craft turns a simple message, a photo, and a special occasion into a digital experience made to be remembered.

What Wish Craft is about

We believe the smallest thoughtful gestures can carry a lot of meaning. Wish Craft is built to make those gestures easy to create, beautiful to open, and simple to share with one link.

What you can create

  • Personalized birthday wishes and celebrations
  • Anniversary and romantic messages
  • Thank-you notes and thoughtful cards
  • Special-occasion digital experiences and downloadable cards

Designed for real moments

Our flows are mobile-first and intentionally guided, so you can focus on the feeling instead of figuring out complicated design tools.

Shared wishes can also expire, helping personal moments stay personal.

Built with care

Wish Craft is an independent project designed and crafted by Aanand Morya. The goal is simple: give people a polished little place to say something that matters.
