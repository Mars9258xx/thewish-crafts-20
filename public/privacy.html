Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Wish Craft (“we”, “our”, or “the service”) helps people create personalized digital wishes, cards and shareable experiences. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to provide the service:

  • Wish content you provide — names, messages, occasion details, optional photos, and other text you enter while creating a wish.
  • Technical data — basic server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) for security, abuse prevention and performance.
  • Cookies / local storage — may be used for session preferences or to remember temporary draft data in your browser.

We do not ask for passwords, payment card numbers, government IDs, or other highly sensitive personal data in the wish forms. Please do not enter such information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To generate and display your personalized wish or card.
  • To create a temporary shareable link (subject to the expiry you choose or the default setting).
  • To store the wish and any uploaded photo for the lifetime of that link so the recipient can view it.
  • To improve the service, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

3. Storage and Retention

When you choose to save and share a wish, the content and any photo may be stored on our servers (currently using secure cloud storage). Wishes typically expire after a set period (for example 3 days by default). After expiry, we aim to delete the associated data and photos. Incomplete or abandoned drafts may be cleaned up earlier.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Infrastructure providers (hosting, storage, CDN) that process data on our behalf under contractual obligations.
  • Authorities if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Anyone you share a wish link with can view the content of that specific wish.

5. Advertising and Analytics

Advertising is currently disabled by default. If we enable third-party advertising (for example Google AdSense) or analytics in the future:

  • Those providers may set cookies or collect usage data according to their own policies.
  • We will update this Privacy Policy with the specific providers and links to their privacy information.
  • You may be able to manage ad personalization through your browser settings or the provider’s tools.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can choose not to upload a photo.
  • You control what text and personal details you put into a wish.
  • If you need a wish or photo removed before expiry, contact us with the share URL.

7. Children’s Privacy

Wish Craft is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or reports, please use the email address listed on our Contact page. Include the wish link if relevant, and never send passwords or secrets.
